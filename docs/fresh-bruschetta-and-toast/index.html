Fresh Bruschetta and Toast

Published on February 22, 2015

An easy appetizer that is delish!

Ingredients

  • 6 tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil
  • 1T olive oil
  • salt to taste
  • baguette (french or sourdough)

Instructions

Dice the tomatoes and garlic, and chop the basil. Toss together with olive oil and a little salt to taste.

Slice the baguette diagonally, brush with olive oil, and toast.

Assemble together on a nice plate with a spoon so folks can top their own toast!
