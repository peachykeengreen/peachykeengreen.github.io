Judi's Chocolately Walnut Crust

Published on August 30, 2016 | Updated on August 30, 2022

Yummy chocolately walnut crust from Judi (bottom crust only); try with pumpkin pie or pudding pie.

Ingredients

  • Chocolately walnut crust (1 crust)2 C walnuts
  • 6 dates + 2 T maple syrup or some combo
  • 1 t vanilla
  • pinch salt
  • 1/4 C cocoa

Instructions

Mix ingredients in food processor. Press into pie plate. Bake 10 min at 350 and then remove and let cool.
