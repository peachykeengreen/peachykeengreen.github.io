About

Published on August 12, 2026

A former techie by day, and like to cook, sing, and dance IRL. I blog vegan recipes, own a farm with my brother, and try to be eco, health, and climate conscious. So “green” has many meanings to me – even if here it’s mostly about plant-based recipes. Check out my farm blog and take a virtual ride in a combine!

Below are a few sources I like for health, nutrition, and inspiration.

Recipe Blogs

  • Post Punk Kitchen - vegan recipes by Isa Chandra Moskowitz (and see cookbooks, below)
  • Oh She Glows - vegan recipes by Angela Liddon (also has a cookbook)
  • Vegan Richa - Indian vegan recipes!

Cookbooks

  • Isa Does It - My favorite vegan cookbook, by Isa
  • Vegan with a Vengeance - Another awesome cookbook, also by Isa

Health

  • NutritionFacts.org - Dr. Greger’s videos and posts on the latest scientific research on nutrition.
  • Articles in PubMed if I need to dig deeper into something. Peer-reviewed, quality-controlled; only journals that meet NIH and NLM scientific standards are indexed.
