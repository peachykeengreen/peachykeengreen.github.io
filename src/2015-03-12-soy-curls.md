I made her [vegan bacon](http://veryveryverygreen.blogspot.com/2014/06/vegan-umami.html) version of these and liked them; Larry thought they were too sweet. Next time I'll cut down the maple syrup.

Judi gave me a bite of her [Indian fusion](http://veryveryverygreen.blogspot.com/2014/05/indian-fusion.html) recipe with soy curls (she raves about that one) and it was very good. I need to try making that one, or use them in another Indian dish; no sweetness there!
