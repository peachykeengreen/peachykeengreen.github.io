It's blackberry and pear season! After enjoying our friend [Judi's "momba" smoothies](https://veryveryverygreen.blogspot.com/2014/06/momba-v2.html) so many times, we got a good Ninja blender to make our own. Dr. Gregor has some [videos about research](http://nutritionfacts.org/video/green-smoothies-what-does-the-science-say/) that shows you get all the benefits of the kale and berries in blended form, but that's not true for all fruits. We harvest a lot of blackberries and pears in August, and end up freezing some of the blackberries. In addition to [pear & blackberry crisp](https://peachykeengreen.com/berry-crisp), it's a great, healthy way to use up the harvest.

## Ingredients

- banana (fresh or frozen)
- carrots (1 or 2 small)
- apple or pear, cored
- dates (2-3)
- soy or almond milk (maybe a cup?)
- frozen berries of your choice (1-2 cups)
- orange, peeled (optional)
- kale (5-10 leaves, optional)

## Instructions

Put everything in a powerful blender and pulse until mixed.  With lots of blackberries, it's pretty purple. It can turn green if we go heavy on the kale and lighter on the berries. It's a great way to use up our blackberry and pear harvests in August.

Makes 2-3 glasses, and keeps fine in the fridge until the next day if you can't drink it all.
