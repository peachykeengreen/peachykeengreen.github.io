A former techie by day, and like to cook, sing, and dance IRL. I blog vegan recipes, own a farm with my brother, and try to be eco, health, and climate conscious. So "green" has many meanings to me -- even if here it's mostly about plant-based recipes. Check out my [farm blog](https://schankfarm.com/) and take a virtual ride in a combine!

Below are a few sources I like for health, nutrition, and inspiration.

## Recipe Blogs

- [Post Punk Kitchen](http://www.theppk.com/) - vegan recipes by Isa Chandra Moskowitz (and see cookbooks, below)
- [Oh She Glows](http://ohsheglows.com/) - vegan recipes by Angela Liddon (also has a cookbook)
- [Vegan Richa](https://www.veganricha.com/) - Indian vegan recipes!

## Cookbooks

- [Isa Does It](http://www.theppk.com/books/isa-does-it/) - My favorite vegan cookbook, by Isa
- [Vegan with a Vengeance](http://www.theppk.com/books/vegan-with-a-vengeance/) - Another awesome cookbook, also by Isa

## Health

- [NutritionFacts.org](http://nutritionfacts.org/) - Dr. Greger's videos and posts on the latest scientific research on nutrition.
- Articles in [PubMed](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed) if I need to dig deeper into something. Peer-reviewed, quality-controlled; only journals that meet [NIH](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) and [NLM](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Library_of_Medicine) scientific standards are indexed.
