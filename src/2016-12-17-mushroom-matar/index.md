I had a delicious mushroom matar at an Indian restaurant, and since then have tried a couple of recipes, including one with cashew cream and one with coconut. Below is a slightly adapted version of a vegan [Mushroom Mattar Methi Malai by Heartbeets](http://myheartbeets.com/mushroom-mattar-methi-malai/) with a few minor tweaks, like adding fresh garbanzos. I liked it, but Larry wanted more flavor (and he added salt, as usual; I'm very light with salt). I wanted more sauce and less garbanzos (I added almost 2 cups, which dominated the dish; 1 cup of garbanzos would have been plenty). Maybe more tomato and cumin and/or coriander next time? The peppers I used weren't very hot, either. There was enough coconut.

My friend Shuchi recommended a super-easy masala: Buy frozen [Trader Joes Channa Masala](http://www.thedailymeal.com/taste-test-trader-joe-s-channa-masala) and add another can of garbanzo beans (the TJ version has [good reviews](http://www.thedailymeal.com/taste-test-trader-joe-s-channa-masala) but is a little too saucy), and top with cilantro. So I mixed the leftovers from our meal with the TJ version,  and that was a good way to finish it off: More sauce, stronger flavor, and the right amount of garbanzos.

## Ingredients

- 3 T olive oil
- 16 oz mushrooms (2+ cups), sliced
- 1 onion
- small chili pepper
- 1 inch ginger
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/2 -1 cup tomatoes (fresh or canned)
- 1 cup coconut milk (reg or light; not cream)
- 1 cup green peas
- 1 cup garbanzo beans (optional)
- Chopped cilantro for garnish (optional)
### Spices

- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp fenugreek
- 1/2 - 1 tsp salt
- black pepper to taste

## Instructions

Put onions, ginger, chili pepper, and garlic in a food processor and puree until smooth. Set aside. Optionally puree tomatoes and set aside.

Heat 1 T oil in pan, and saute mushrooms for 7-8 minutes or until they soften. Set aside in a bowl.

Heat remaining 2 T oil in pan and add blended onion mixture. Saute 10 minutes or until onions are cooked through. Add spices and stir for 30 seconds to toast. Add tomatoes and cook 4-5 minutes. Stir in peas, mushrooms, garbanzos, and coconut milk. Bring to a boil, stirring. Garnish with cilantro.

Serve over riced cauliflower or rice, or with vegan naan.
