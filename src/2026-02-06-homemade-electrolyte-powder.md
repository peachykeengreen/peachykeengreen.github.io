Larry plays tennis most weekends, and looked into making electrolyte powder to mix into his water bottle. He adapted this [electrolyte powder recipe](https://elanaspantry.com/homemade-electrolyte-powder/) to make a small jar, which lasts him about 10 months when used once a week. It's a gazillion times cheaper than pre-made powders, and has all the essential contents.

Makes about 40 large bottles (24-oz) of electrolyte water.

- 1/3 C sugar (or more to taste)
- Flavoring (optional): lemon, lime, cranberry or other fruit powder, etc.

## Instructions

Combine sugar, salt, potassium, and magnesium powders in a pint mason jar and shake well.

To make an electrolyte drink, place 1 teaspoon electrolyte powder in 24 oz (4 cups) water and shake vigorously.

Add any flavoring, as desired.

Drink up!
