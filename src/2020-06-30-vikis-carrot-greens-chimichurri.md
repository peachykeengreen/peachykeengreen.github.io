Viki brought her adaptation of a [carrot-top chimichurri](https://www.loveandlemons.com/carrot-greens-chimichurri/) to our outdoor distance gathering, and I fell in love with it! The tang and garlic are amazing on bread, or as a salad dressing (see Viki's salad, below). I made a pesto with carrot tops a couple of months ago and it was meh... After that, I just ended up throwing carrot tops in soup (it was great in [split pea soup)](https://peachykeengreen.blogspot.com/2017/03/split-pea-soup.html). But next time I get farmer's market carrots, I'm definitely making this!

## Ingredients

- 1 C finely chopped carrot greens
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp sweet paprika (that is, regular, not smoked)
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 T rice vinegar
- 2 T red wine vinegar
- 1/4 C extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions

In a small bowl, combine the carrot greens, oregano, cumin, paprika, red pepper flakes, garlic, salt, and pepper. Stir in the vinegar and olive oil. Taste and adjust seasonings.
