Shelley and Michael had us over for lunch and served us the most delicious, thick carrot leek soup, sourdough bread (City Bread from [Cheeseboard](http://cheeseboardcollective.coop/bakery-cheese/breads-index/)) and salad (a mix of leaves, garbanzos, red peppers,  avocado, and more, with a thick balsamic oil dressing). Shelley said the soup recipe was from [Fields of Greens](https://www.amazon.com/Fields-Greens-Vegetarian-Celebrated-Restaurant/dp/0553091395).  I found the [recipe](https://keils.com/collection/soup/?recipe_id=54100) on the web! Here's a somewhat-adapted, easier version.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium onions or 1 large leek (2 C), chopped
- 1.5 lb potatoes (2-3 cups), chopped
- 1.5 lb carrots (2-3 cups), chopped
- 1 medium sweet potato/yam (2+ C), chopped
- 3 cloves garlic (no need to chop)
- 2 T oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes or diced hot pepper.
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and pepper
- 6 C vegetable stock (water + Better than Bouillon)

Optional toppings:
- 1 T chopped fresh parsley or thyme  
- 1 T cashew cream

## Instructions

In a large soup pot, add oil and hot pepper. 

Option 1 (Roast veggies): Shake onion, potatos, carrots, yam, and garlic 
in a bag with some oil and salt, and roast in the oven for 40 minutes at 350 degrees.
Let cool, then add to the soup pot.

Option 2 (Saute veggies): Saute onions, hot pepper, and salt for 5 minutes in soup pot. Add carrots, potato, sweet potato, and garlic, and saute another 5-10 minutes.

Once the veggies are mostly cooked, add thyme and pepper, and saute another 5 minutes.

Add stock bring the soup to a gentle boil. Reduce heat and simmer, covered, for about 30 minutes, until the carrots are very tender. Let cool and puree.

Garnish each serving with a swirl of cashew cream and sprinkle of fresh thyme or parsley.

Make 9 to 10 cups.
